# H1 — The rules, written down before we look

*(Drafted 28 September 2026. Awaiting sign-off. **Nothing has been run:** the model has not been
applied to a single non-SH3 domain, and no correlation between a prediction and a measurement outside
SH3 has been computed.)*

## What this file is for

Phase 1's headline is a median per-domain Spearman ρ of **0.839** on 15 held-out SH3 domains, and
Phase 2 measured it against an independent abundance assay at pooled |ρ| **0.600**, close to the
0.709 ceiling set by two assays agreeing with each other. Every one of those numbers is about **one
family**.

H1 asks one question:

> **Does the frozen model reach comparable per-domain accuracy on protein families it has never
> seen?**

The threshold, the arms, the margin and the reading of every outcome are fixed **now**, before any
non-SH3 prediction exists. This file is committed to git first; only then is the notebook written.

Once committed it is never edited. Changes go in a dated amendment, and the original plan's results
are still reported.

## Part 1 — What the H1.0 audit already settled

`phase3/results/H1_0_leakage_audit/` — run before this file, and it changed the design.

The frozen model held out SH3 and trained on everything else in MegaScale, so **any second family
must be shown absent from training before it means anything.** Three facts came out:

1. **MegaScale's 412 training domains are 32–72 residues** (median 51), because the proteolysis assay
   caps construct length there. This is a property of the measurement, not a modelling choice.
2. **PDZ is absent from training — but because it is too long** (70–97 residues, median 88; 1 of 43
   would fit). Evaluating on PDZ alone confounds *new family* with *longer than anything in
   training*.
3. **Three families are absent *and* length-matched**: Myb/SANT (PF00249), PHD (PF00628) and B-box
   (PF00643).

The audit validates itself on SH3: 27 MegaScale domains match PF00018 against the 26 the training
table flags `is_sh3`.

**No retraining is required.** The frozen model is applied unchanged to all arms.

## Part 3 — The arms

| arm | families | verdict? | what it isolates |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — MAIN** | Myb/SANT + PHD + B-box, pooled | **yes** | generality to unseen families, length held constant |
| 2 | PDZ | **no** | family *plus* length extrapolation |
| 3 | SH3, re-derived from E2 | **no — control** | must reproduce E2's per-domain figures |

**Arm 3 is not optional.** If the SH3 per-domain correlations do not reproduce E2's, this
implementation differs from E2 in something other than the family, and arms 1 and 2 are not
interpretable. The run stops. This is the role G1's reproduction check played, and it is why G1's
result could be trusted when it overturned a published claim.

### Why the main arm pools three families

Fifteen domains is not enough, and the power calculation in Part 5 shows it. Pooling the three
length-matched families gives **35 domains**, which is the only way this design resolves anything
finer than "correlated or not". The three are pooled because they share the property under test —
absent from training, length inside the training range — not because pooling improves the number.

**Per-family results are reported separately as well**, always, so that a pooled figure cannot hide
one family carrying the others.

## Part 4 — The decision rule, fixed now

### The statistic

**Median per-domain Spearman |ρ|** between predicted ΔΔG_fold and measured `normalized_fitness`,
over domains with at least 20 measured variants — E2's own convention, so the comparison is like for
like.

### It is a non-inferiority test, not a significance test

"Comparable accuracy" is the claim, and a null-hypothesis test cannot support it: failing to reject
"no difference" is not evidence of similarity. E1 already demonstrated the trap from the other side,
where an underpowered null could not distinguish absence of effect from absence of power.

So the comparison is stated as a margin:

```
Δ = median|ρ|(arm)  −  median|ρ|(SH3, E2's 21 domains)
95 % CI on Δ from 4,000 bootstrap resamples over DOMAINS, not variants
```

| outcome | condition | reading |
|---|---|---|
| **Generality supported** | CI lower bound > **−0.15** | accuracy on unseen families is within one domain-to-domain SD of SH3's |
| **Not supported** | CI upper bound < **−0.15** | materially worse, and reported as such |
| **Inconclusive** | CI spans −0.15 | the design could not resolve it, and says so |

### Where −0.15 comes from

**It is the standard deviation of SH3's own per-domain |ρ|, 0.140, rounded.** A family whose median
sits within one domain-to-domain SD of SH3's is doing what SH3 domains do among themselves. The
number is taken from `per_domain_E2.csv`, which is published, and is fixed here because any margin
chosen after seeing Δ would be worth nothing.

### Minimum

Fewer than **20 domains** with ≥ 20 measured variants in the main arm → numbers are printed, **no
verdict**.

## Part 5 — Power, stated in advance

Computed by resampling SH3's own observed per-domain |ρ| spread (21 domains, median 0.573,
SD 0.140), so it asks: *if a new family behaved exactly like SH3, how precisely would we know?*

| arm composition | domains | 95 % CI on the median | width |
|---|---|---|---|
| Myb/SANT alone | 15 | 0.462 – 0.705 | **0.243** |
| Myb/SANT + PHD | 26 | 0.491 – 0.675 | 0.184 |
| **Myb/SANT + PHD + B-box** | **35** | **0.508 – 0.656** | **0.148** |
| PDZ | 31 | 0.491 – 0.656 | 0.166 |
| SH3 reference | 21 | 0.491 – 0.675 | 0.184 |

**This is why the main arm pools three families.** At 15 domains the interval is wider than the
margin it would be compared against, so the answer would have been "inconclusive" whatever the data
said — a guaranteed non-result, visible only because the calculation was done first.

At 35 domains the interval is **tighter than SH3's own reference arm**, and comparable to the 0.15
margin. The design can therefore distinguish *comparable* from *materially worse*. It cannot resolve
a difference of 0.05, and no claim of that precision will be made.

## Part 6 — How the two arms are read together

Fixed now, because this 2 × 2 is the whole reason PDZ is included.

| main (length-matched) | PDZ | reading |
|---|---|---|
| supported | supported | **generality supported**, and length is not a limit either |
| supported | not supported | **the limit is construct length, not family** — the most informative outcome, and it is available only because both arms run |
| not supported | not supported | generality not supported. The length confound cannot be blamed, because the main arm is length-matched |
| not supported | supported | unexpected. Reported as observed, **no claim either way**, and it would mean the design is measuring something not yet identified |

**PDZ carries no verdict of its own** under any of these. Its role is the contrast.

## Part 7 — Known weaknesses, admitted in advance

- **There is no noise ceiling for the new families.** E2 could say 0.600 was 85 % of what two assays
  achieve against each other, because SH3 had both MegaScale and Domainome measurements. The new
  families have no MegaScale data **by construction** — that is why they are held out. A
  within-assay bound from Domainome's replicate `fitness_sigma` is reported instead, and it is a
  weaker quantity that must not be described as a ceiling in the E2 sense.
- **Assay quality may differ by family.** A lower |ρ| could reflect noisier aPCA measurements rather
  than worse predictions, and the within-assay bound only partly addresses it.
- **Three families pooled are still three families.** Myb/SANT, PHD and B-box are all small
  zinc-binding or DNA-binding folds, which is not a random sample of protein space.
- **This tests folding, not binding.** Phase 1's ~80-position threshold is a binding claim on
  PSD95-PDZ3. H1 says nothing about it, and the result must not be extended to it.
- **B-box and PHD coordinate zinc.** Their stability in an abundance assay may depend on metal
  availability in a way the model cannot represent and SH3 never required. If the main arm fails,
  this is the first thing to check before concluding anything about generality.
- **Domainome measures abundance, not ΔΔG.** The same interpretive gap E2 carried.

Required by the convention that counting is allowed before sign-off but looking at predictions is
not.

**Confirmed.** The work done before this file: the H1.0 leakage audit, which read `domain_key`,
`wt_seq` and `is_sh3` from A2's MegaScale feature table and domain identifiers and sequences from
Domainome — **no prediction column was loaded** — and the group counts in Part 2, which read
`domain_ID`, `wt_aa`, `mut_aa`, `STOP` and `normalized_fitness` from Domainome Table 2.

The power table in Part 5 was computed from `per_domain_E2.csv`, which is a **published Phase 2
result about SH3**, resampled to ask how precisely 35 domains would be known. It used no non-SH3
prediction, because none exists.

**The model has not been run on any non-SH3 domain.** No ΔΔG has been computed for a Myb/SANT, PHD,
B-box or PDZ variant, and no correlation between prediction and measurement outside SH3 has been
calculated or displayed.

One caveat, stated rather than glossed: E2's SH3 per-domain correlations are published and known to
be a median |ρ| of 0.573 across 21 domains. That number is the comparator, so it is necessarily known
in advance. The guarantee is that **nothing on the other side of the comparison exists yet.**
